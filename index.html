Welcome to Swami Dayananda College of Arts & Science You tube Channel. Manjakkudi is a small village situated on the banks of Kaveri in Tamilnadu. A silent educational and community empowerment revolution has been in progress in this village for over 20 years now. Manjakkudi is the birthplace of Pujya Swami Dayananda Saraswati, a world renowned Vedanta scholar and Spiritual teacher

Inspired by Swamiji's vision that the youth of our nation should receive value education and cultural validation, SDET(Swami Dayananda Educational Trust) has been at the forefront to help realize our Founder's dream. Since 2003, SDET has been running two schools and a college in the region.
